2. Executive Summary

The meeting was held to coordinate the preparation, submission, and scope of the Plumbing, Electrical, and Mechanical (MEP) Construction Drawings for the CONVENGERS BAHAY BUILD project.

The Plumbing Construction Drawings are to be expedited for submission by Thursday, November 19, 2026, while the Electrical and Mechanical Construction Drawings are targeted for submission by Friday or Saturday, November 20–21, 2026. The termite-related plumbing layout was clarified as outside the scope of work of the Ingeniare Team. The meeting agreed to proceed with the current MEP proposals, with any proposed changes, revisions, or adjustments to be coordinated by December 4, 2026. Finalization is subject to coordination with Ar. Dax and Idr. Zach.
